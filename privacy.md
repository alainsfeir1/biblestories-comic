# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy covers Bible Stories Publisher, a private tool used by the owner of Greatest Bible Stories Comic to publish the owner's own videos and images to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

## What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to the owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube API Services, and the owner's own Instagram account, through the Instagram API. It uses that access only to upload and publish the owner's content and to check that uploads succeeded.

## What the tool does not do

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers, followers or anyone else. It has no sign-up, no user accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

## Data storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's content. Access can be revoked at any time from [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from Meta Business settings.

## YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
